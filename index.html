Quantum Core

Inside the Atom

Wake Max back up — one fundamental particle at a time.

See how it works

Quantum Core is an interactive experience where you explore an atom from the inside to bring an offline intelligence — Max — back online. Every particle you discover restores a piece of the system: the electron, the photon, the quarks, the gluon, the W and Z bosons. Along the way, you learn the real structure of matter, one honest, well-explained beat at a time.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

🛡️
No data collected

Quantum Core runs entirely on your device and works fully offline. It has no network access at all.

Overview

Quantum Core does not collect any data. The App runs entirely on your device, works fully offline, and never sends any information anywhere. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no analytics.

Information We Collect

None. Quantum Core makes no network connections. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. Nothing you do in the App ever leaves your device.

Analytics & Tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Third-Party Services

Quantum Core does not integrate any third-party services. It is distributed through the App Store; any information related to that download is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by the App. If you have enabled crash sharing in your device settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous, aggregated crash reports — this is controlled entirely by Apple and your own settings, never by the App.

Children's Privacy

Quantum Core is suitable for all ages. Because the App collects no data at all, it collects no information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the App's data practices ever change, this page will be updated with a new "Last updated" date.
