Overview

Quantum Core ("the App") is developed by Marlon Ribas ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Quantum Core is designed to work with minimal data collection. Depending on the version of the App and platform features you use, we may collect:

  • Basic device and usage information (such as device model, OS version, and crash diagnostics) used to improve stability and performance.
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics about how features are used within the App (for example, which particles are discovered).
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support, such as your email address and the content of your message.

Quantum Core does not require an account to use, and we do not knowingly collect names, precise location, or payment information through the App itself.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App's features and stability.
  • Diagnose technical issues and fix bugs.
  • Respond to support requests sent to our contact email.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

The App may rely on platform-level services provided by Apple (such as App Store distribution and crash reporting) to operate. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We retain the limited information described above only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.

Children's Privacy

Quantum Core is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information, if any, we hold about you, or to request that it be deleted, using the contact details below.
